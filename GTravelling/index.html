Adventure Experiences

Fuel your adrenaline across alpine ridgelines, crystal reef depths, and untamed jungles.

Altitude Trail

Trekking

Traverse scenic passes like Triund, Kedarkantha, and Valley of Flowers with breathtaking views.

Season: Apr - Jun & Sep - Nov Level: Moderate
Under The Stars

Camping

Pitch your tent alongside turquoise rivers in Rishikesh or gaze upon the Milky Way in Spiti.

Season: Year-round Level: Easy
Ocean Depths

Scuba Diving

Plunge into pristine coral gardens in Havelock and Netrani to swim with manta rays and turtles.

Season: Oct - May Level: Beginner to Pro
Aerial Heights

Mountain Adventures

Soar high while paragliding in Bir Billing, rock climb in Hampi, or ski down snowy slopes in Solang.

Season: Oct - Mar Level: Moderate
Wild Habitats

Wildlife Safari

Track royal Bengal tigers in Ranthambore and Jim Corbett, and spot one-horned rhinos in Kaziranga.

Season: Nov - Jun Level: All Ages
Open Highway

Road Trips

Cruise the world-famous Manali-Leh highway, the Konkan coastal stretch, or the Western Ghats passes.
